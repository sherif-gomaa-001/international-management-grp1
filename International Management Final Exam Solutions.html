And why does the case evidence support that classification?

Classification

Global Company Model

Flexit is operating on a strict Global Company model.

Decisions are highly centralized at the home-country headquarters, focusing strictly on exporting home-country products.

Validation from the case

  • Highly centralized decisions at the home-country headquarters.
  • Strict focus on exporting home-country products.
  • 100% centralized US manufacturing — all production decisions stay at home.

Q12Part 2 · Generic International Management

Three Strategies for International Organisations

Based on the course's core structural frameworks, organisations can adopt distinct approaches depending on their goals regarding competition and societal impact.

B

Blue Ocean Strategy

Creating an entirely new, uncontested market space where there is no existing competition — a "monopoly strategy". Instead of fighting over existing customers, companies create new demand and make the competition irrelevant.

R

Bloody Red Ocean Strategy

A highly aggressive approach where companies compete in existing, crowded markets. Cut-throat competition — likened to sharks fighting, turning the ocean red — where the main goal is simply to beat rivals and exploit existing demand.

W

White Ocean Strategy

Prioritises corporate social responsibility (CSR) and community welfare over pure profit. Focuses on social impact and gaining long-term trust by investing in the community rather than trying to win market share through aggressive tactics.

Q6Part 3 · Culture

Cultural Sensitivity in Western Media

What challenges has Salah faced in terms of cultural sensitivity — and how can international companies ensure their leaders are culturally sensitive in global markets?

Challenges faced

Four real pressure points

  • 'Greedy' label (2021–22) — asking for a pay rise was framed as greed on social media and in parts of the press; Abbas noted 'asking for money... is a sin' in public opinion.
  • Faith under the microscope — questions on Ramadan fasting and his visible prayer celebration, in a country where Islamophobia had been rising since 2001.
  • Politics — a dual-audience trap — in Oct 2023 he was criticised in Egypt and the Arab world for ~11 days of silence on Gaza; when he said 'all lives are sacred' some called it too neutral, while in Western media any statement risked being read as political.
  • Directness vs. club culture (Dec 2025) — his public 'thrown under the bus' interview after being benched by Arne Slot was framed in British media as disloyal and ego-driven, while many Arab fans saw it as defending his dignity.
The remedy

How companies build sensitivity

  • Pre-assignment cultural training using Hofstede (power distance, individualism, masculinity, uncertainty avoidance) and Trompenaars (loyalty vs. rules) — 'study customs, norms, values and practices'.
  • Local advisors and media protocols — like Abbas's network of local lawyers and advisors; prepare statements for sensitive topics in each market before a crisis happens.
  • Diverse leadership teams and feedback — avoid the professor's 'major mistakes': cultural bias, blindness to our own norms, seeing other cultures as 'deviations', and stereotyping.
  • Clear values and crisis-communication rules — decide in advance which causes the brand speaks on, with consistent words and actions ('walk the talk' — Kotter).

Q13Part 3 · Change Management

The Comfort Circle

Using the Comfort Circle Diagram, compare the move of Mo Salah to Trabzonspor with the move to Fiorentina.

Fiorentina 2015 · age 22

A career-rescue push

Comfort: Chelsea bench (only 13 apps).

Fear: New country, new league.

Learning: Italian language, adapting to Serie A tactics.

Growth: 7B assists/goals; hero on a Champions League side.

Push move — forced out of a stalled comfort zone (career rescue).

Trabzonspor 2026 · age 34

A strategic late-career pull + push

Comfort: Liverpool — 9 years, family settled.

Fear: Lost future, bench, unfamiliar language, fan pressure.

Learning: New league, coach, teammates.

Growth: Club icon, Europa League, brand reach (Turkish TV + MENA).

Pull + push — leaves a deep comfort zone at career peak to take the next stage.

Conclusion — Fiorentina was a career-rescue move out of a failing comfort zone; Trabzonspor is a strategic late-career move out of a very successful one. The first risked his future; the second risks his legacy and brand visibility — but offers leadership and new-market growth. Both follow O'Toole (homeostasis) and Lewin (unfreeze–change–refreeze): growth only happens after leaving the comfort zone and passing through fear and learning.

Q20Part 3 · Change Management

Trabzonspor Move — Top 3 Challenges

What are the top 3 expected challenges Salah might face in his recent move to the Turkish team — and who manages each one?

Based on the 2026 data indicating Salah's free transfer to Trabzonspor following a fallout with coach Arne Slot, we can evaluate his challenges through the lens of Change Management and International Business structural shifts.

1

Homeostasis & Family Disruption

Analysis: According to James O'Toole's concept of homeostasis, humans naturally resist leaving their "Comfort Zone." Salah spent nearly a decade in England, where his children were born and attended school. A move to Turkey creates massive personal and familial upheaval, triggering a "Culture Shock" transition.

Managed by: Salah and Ramy Abbas. Abbas handles all off-field logistics and legalities (as he did during the Fiorentina loan) to minimise friction, while Salah and his family actively manage their cultural acclimation.

2

New Team Dynamics & Leadership

Analysis: Following a public fallout with Arne Slot, Salah must go through Lewin's "Unfreezing" process to let go of old expectations and successfully enter the "Changing" phase under Trabzonspor's coach, Thomas Reis. He has to adapt to a completely new tactical system and locker-room culture in the Süper Lig.

Managed by: Salah and Coach Thomas Reis. The case notes that Abbas does not interfere with football matters ("Mohamed knows his football") — Salah must use his own emotional intelligence to buy into Reis's vision.

3

Financial Value in a Lower-Revenue League

Analysis: The Turkish Süper Lig does not command the massive broadcasting and commercial revenues of the English Premier League (as evidenced by Deloitte Money League data). Replicating a contract near his previous targets (€54–62M) will be highly challenging — requiring a heavy shift toward performance variables and international image-rights exploitation.

Managed by: Ramy Abbas (Magic Circle). Abbas legally controls the non-UK image-rights company and negotiates the fixed vs. variable pay splits — creatively structuring Salah's commercial and playing contracts to protect his premium brand value despite operating in a less wealthy league.
